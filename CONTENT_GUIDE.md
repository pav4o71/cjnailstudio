# Content Guide

## Voice

Warm, feminine, welcoming, playful and clear. Prefer concise English, concrete service guidance and confident calls to action. Emoji may appear in social-derived accents but should not reduce website clarity.

## Proof before claims

Use real work, reviews, hygiene evidence and location details. Do not use unsupported superlatives or promise outcomes that sources cannot prove.

## Calls to action

Primary: Book. Secondary: WhatsApp, Call, View services, Get directions. Walk-in availability must not imply guaranteed immediate service.

## Unknowns

Use visible TBD markers in working content. Never publish TBD or guessed business policy.
