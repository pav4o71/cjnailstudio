## 2. Strategic recommendation

### Business objective

Turn social and local-search discovery into a confident booking handoff on mobile, while keeping WhatsApp, phone and walk-in alternatives visible. Booking is the approved primary conversion (**D-002**); WhatsApp and walk-ins must remain visible (**D-003**).

### Brand opportunity

Beauty Nail Studio by Cj can own the space between a portfolio-led social account and a clear local service website:

> **Bring the look you have in mind. Find the right nail or lash service, see real work and contact the Knightsbridge studio without friction.** **[AI]**

### What the brand should lead with

1. **Artistry made understandable.** Lead with custom nail art and a result-led service chooser rather than salon jargon alone (`instagram-profile`, **[VF]**; `google-maps`, **[CO]**; strategy conclusion **[AI]**).
2. **Care made visible.** Show process and hygiene evidence. An official post says tools are cleaned, disinfected and sanitized after every client (`facebook-profile`, **[OC]**). Customer comments about careful removal, cleanliness or autoclave use must remain attributed opinions (`google-maps`, **[CO]**).
3. **A warm, local welcome.** Use concise, friendly guidance and real Knightsbridge information. The current approved location is G/F Unit R19, Knightsbridge Residences, Makati City 1210 (`facebook-profile`, **[VF]**; **D-001**).
4. **Low-friction contact.** Provide a persistent Book action plus WhatsApp/phone and walk-in alternatives (`facebook-profile`, `instagram-profile`, **[VF]**; **D-002**, **D-003**).

### What the brand must not lead with yet

- **Matcha as an operating promise.** The nail/lash-plus-Matcha concept has been promoted (`instagram-profile`, **[VF]**), but its current status, menu, hours and appointment relationship are unresolved (**D-005**). It is a potential differentiator, not launch-safe copy.

- **“Best,” “luxury,” “guaranteed,” “long-lasting” or medical-grade claims.** The evidence does not substantiate these as business promises.

## 6. Voice and editorial system

### Voice attributes

- **Warm, not vague:** “Tell us the look you have in mind” rather than “Indulge in unparalleled luxury.”
- **Feminine, not exclusionary:** soft, polished and playful language without assuming every client identifies a particular way.
- **Confident, not absolute:** state verified services and contact details; attribute opinions and avoid guarantees.
- **Helpful, not jargon-heavy:** pair terms such as BIAB with owner-approved plain-language explanations when supplied.
- **Locally grounded, not keyword-stuffed:** use “Knightsbridge, Makati” naturally in titles, headings and visit information.
- **Concise and conversion-oriented:** short sentences, descriptive CTA labels and practical information close to the action.

These traits synthesize official captions described as warm, feminine, informal, emoji-rich and invitation-led (`facebook-profile`, `instagram-profile`, **[OC]**; tone synthesis **[AI]**).

### Style rules

1. Use sentence case for headings and buttons.
2. Prefer “you” and “we”; use “clients” when discussing policy.
3. Keep emojis to optional campaign accents. Do not use them as icons, labels or the only meaning carrier.
4. Write dates explicitly and show expiry dates for promotions.
5. Use “starting at,” “from” or duration language only after owner-confirmed data exists.
6. Attribute reviews with platform and date; use approved excerpts only, not reconstructed quotations from paraphrases.
7. Write phone, address and hours from one canonical content record.
8. Never imply that walk-in acceptance equals guaranteed capacity.

### Phrase bank

**Recommended:** “Bring your inspiration”; “Find your service”; “See recent nail art”; “Book or message us”; “Open daily in Knightsbridge, Makati”; “Walk-ins accepted”; “Not sure what to choose? Message the studio.” **[AI]**

**Avoid:** “the best nail salon in Makati”; “flawless every time”; “sterile/medical-grade”; “instant booking”; “same-day availability”; “affordable”; “luxury”; “cash and GCash only”; “cards not accepted”; “10% off” without a currently approved campaign.

### Post-launch KPI framework

Collect a 30-day baseline before setting uplift targets:

1. **Primary:** booking-intent rate = unique sessions with `booking_handoff_started`, `whatsapp_click` or `phone_click` ÷ qualified sessions.

3. **Local intent:** organic visits to `/visit`, directions clicks and phone/WhatsApp actions from local landing pages.
4. **Content utility:** service/category views that proceed to Gallery, Book or contact.
5. **Failure:** booking-handoff failure rate and fallback-use rate.

After the baseline, the owner and integrator should set numeric targets by channel and exclude bots/internal traffic. A target invented before baseline would not be evidence-based.
