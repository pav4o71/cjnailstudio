# Master Brief

Status: WORKING BASELINE

## Goal

Design and build a distinctive, mobile-first website for Beauty Nail Studio by Cj that turns social and local-search discovery into confident bookings. Booking is the primary conversion; WhatsApp, phone and walk-ins remain supported.

## Verified business baseline

- Current public location: G/F Unit R19, Knightsbridge Residences, Makati City.
- Open daily from 12:00 noon to 9:00 PM.
- Services publicly mentioned: nails, custom nail art, extensions, BIAB/infill, soft gel and lashes.
- Current booking paths: WhatsApp/phone, Facebook/Instagram messages and walk-ins.
- Google Maps showed a strong rating and review volume at research capture.
- Reviews repeatedly support custom-design accuracy, named technician skill, hygiene, hospitality and value.

## Positioning direction

Approachable Makati custom-art and lash studio combining artistry, careful service, hygiene and a warm self-care atmosphere. The nail/lash-plus-matcha concept is the strongest potential differentiator, pending owner verification.

## Experience principles

1. Booking must be reachable from every important mobile screen.
2. Help clients choose by desired result, not salon terminology alone.
3. Connect gallery examples to the correct service category.
4. Use real work, reviews, technician expertise and hygiene as proof.
5. Preserve the pink-led playful femininity while improving hierarchy and restraint.
6. Keep information clear for residents, Makati workers and visitors.

## Required outputs

- Website strategy and measurable success criteria.
- Sitemap, page responsibilities and conversion paths.
- Design system and responsive component guidance.
- Content model and page-level copy requirements.
- Media placement and editing plan using media IDs.
- Booking/payment architecture after P0 requirements are answered.
- Final Codex rules, implementation plan and acceptance tests.

## P0 unknowns

- Official service menu, prices, durations and add-ons.
- Staff roster, specialties, schedules and capacity.
- Booking buffers, walk-in allocation and double-booking rules.
- Cancellation, rescheduling, late/no-show and deposit policies.
- Confirmed payment methods, refunds and reconciliation.
- Matcha-café status, menu, hours and relationship to appointments.

## Prohibited shortcuts

- Do not select a booking/payment provider before evaluating confirmed requirements.
- Do not publish customer images without consent confirmation.
- Do not treat legacy locations as current branches without owner confirmation.
- Do not copy competitor designs, text or images.
