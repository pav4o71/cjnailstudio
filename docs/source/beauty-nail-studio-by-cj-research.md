# Beauty Nail Studio by Cj — Business, Brand and Website Research

_Captured 17 August 2026. Public-source research only._

## Executive readout

Beauty Nail Studio by Cj is a newly opened Knightsbridge, Makati nail-and-lash studio with a strong custom-art reputation and an emerging nail/lash-plus-matcha-café concept. The clearest conversion opportunity is to turn a socially discovered, message-based business into a booking-first website without losing its personal, friendly character.

The evidence supports four primary brand promises:

1. **Artistry:** customers repeatedly praise accurate reproduction of inspiration images and distinctive custom sets.
2. **Care:** official content and reviews emphasize cleanliness, sterilization, careful removal and attention to detail.
3. **Welcome:** the studio uses warm language and reviews mention hospitality touches such as water, snacks and slippers.
4. **Convenience:** daily hours, walk-ins, WhatsApp, Instagram and a central Knightsbridge location lower friction, but there is no visible first-party booking engine.

The current website strategy should therefore lead with **“show us the look; we’ll help you book the right service”**, supported by real work, technician expertise, hygiene proof, transparent services and a persistent booking action.

## Voice, tone and content system

The voice is warm, feminine, informal and conversion-oriented. Captions are short, emoji-rich and repeatedly restate hours, location, phone and a booking/walk-in invitation. Emotional language centers on self-care, confidence, beauty, welcome and pampering; practical proof centers on nail retention, precision, hygiene and availability.

Recurring content pillars:

- Finished custom nail art and seasonal inspiration.
- Nail process, retention and value.
- Lash transformations.
- Hygiene and tool sanitation.
- Studio experience, team and location.
- Promotions, holidays and new-client offers.
- Loyalty and repeat visits.
- Matcha-café differentiation.

The strongest useful phrases and ideas are “self-care and confidence,” “trust the process,” “book now or walk in,” “bring your inspiration,” “custom nail art,” “BIAB,” and the Makati/Knightsbridge location.

## Reputation and customer insight

Google Maps showed 4.8/5 from 135 reviews at capture. Google labels reviews unverified; the following are synthesized customer themes, not business claims:

- Custom-art accuracy and ability to reproduce inspiration images.
- Repeated praise for technician Rufa; other reviews mention Kris.
- Cleanliness, autoclave/sterility, careful removal and good equipment.
- Friendly, attentive service and comfortable consultation.
- Hospitality touches including water, snacks and slippers.
- Positive lash-extension results and reasonable-value perceptions.
- Discovery through TikTok, Google reviews and proximity while visiting Makati.

These themes should become proof-oriented website modules: review excerpts, team profiles, “bring your inspiration” gallery paths, hygiene process and a clear first-visit guide.

## Booking-readiness assessment

The public evidence is sufficient to design a booking-first information architecture, but not sufficient to implement scheduling or payments safely. Before selecting a widget or building custom booking, the owner must define:

- Service names, durations, prices, add-ons, removals and complexity rules.
- Staff specialties, schedules, capacity and staff-selection behavior.
- Lead time, buffers, walk-in allocation and double-booking prevention.
- Rescheduling, cancellation, no-show, late-arrival and deposit rules.
- Confirmed payment methods, refunds and reconciliation.
- Inspiration-image upload requirements.
- WhatsApp, SMS and email notifications.

Candidate integration categories for the later architecture phase are a specialist booking widget versus custom scheduling; platform calendar versus Google Calendar sync; WhatsApp/SMS/email notifications; platform deposits versus payment links/custom checkout; lightweight CMS versus repository-managed content; and conversion/booking analytics. No provider should be selected until the P0 operational requirements are confirmed.

## Prioritized information gaps

**P0 — blocks booking/payment design**

- Official services, prices, durations and add-ons.
- Staff roster, schedules, specialties and capacity.
- Cancellation, rescheduling, late/no-show and deposit policies.
- Confirmed payment methods, refunds and reconciliation.
- Current matcha-café status, menu, hours and appointment relationship.

**P1 — blocks accurate brand/SEO implementation**

- Relationship among Knightsbridge, Beacon Tower 3, Medical Towers and “Beauty Nail Studio by Cj 2.”
- Original logo, font licenses and formal brand colors.
- High-resolution originals and customer/model releases.
- Official TikTok handle.
- Google Business Profile ownership and future canonical website link.

**P2 — useful growth inputs**

- Loyalty-card terms, gift cards, packages and memberships.
- Parking, building access and accessibility information.
