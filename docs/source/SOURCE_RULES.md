# Source Rules

1. The research dossier, structured JSON dataset and media manifest are the authoritative evidence package.
2. Distinguish verified business facts, customer reviews, third-party claims and analyst inferences.
3. Cite source_id for factual claims and media_id for image decisions.
4. Never invent prices, service durations, staff schedules, availability, booking rules, deposits, payment methods, matcha details or legal claims.
5. If sources conflict, record the conflict in DECISION_LOG.md and request owner confirmation.
6. Do not edit source files. Add dated working or approved documents elsewhere.
7. Signed social-media asset URLs expire; use the retained Drive files as planning assets.
8. Images containing identifiable people require explicit consent review before public use.
9. Competitor material is reference-only and must not be copied.
10. Social metrics and promotions are snapshots, not permanent facts.
