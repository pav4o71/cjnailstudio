Recommended workflow
1. Give every agent the source rules, research dossier, dataset, and master brief.
2. Agents 1–3 produce brand/UX, content/photo, and booking/technical recommendations in their assigned folders.
3. The owner answers the priority booking questions before any booking system is implemented.
4. Agent 4 reconciles conflicts and produces one approved strategy and architecture.
5. Copy the approved Codex build pack into the repository. AGENTS.md must be placed at the repository root before Codex starts implementation.


Four-agent responsibilities
Agent 1 — Brand, UX, visual direction, sitemap, conversion journey.
Agent 2 — Content, SEO, photo selection, placement, rights review, image-edit prompts.
Agent 3 — Booking operations, integration options, payments, privacy, security, acceptance criteria.
Agent 4 — Integration, conflict resolution, final decisions, implementation-ready handoff.


Non-negotiable rules
- Separate verified facts, customer opinions, competitor observations, and analyst inferences.
- Do not invent prices, durations, availability, staffing rules, deposits, payment methods, cancellation rules, or booking logic.
- Treat location details as unconfirmed until the owner resolves the conflicting public signals.
- Do not publish anything in Consent Check Required until rights and consent are documented.
- Strategy and build files marked TEMPLATE or PRELIMINARY are starting structures, not approved decisions.
- Keep booking-first mobile UX, accessibility, privacy, security, and measurable acceptance tests in scope.
- No Drive sharing permissions were changed during setup.

First owner decisions
1. Confirm the current public address and map pin.
2. Confirm the complete service menu, prices, durations, add-ons, and availability.
3. Confirm staff, resources, simultaneous capacity, working hours, buffers, and lead time.
4. Confirm deposit/payment, cancellation, rescheduling, no-show, refund, late-arrival, and walk-in policies.
5. Confirm which photos, logos, reviews, and customer likenesses are licensed for website publication.
